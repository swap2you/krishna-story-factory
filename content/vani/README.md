# Prabhupāda Vāṇī collection (Unified Platform M4)

Private collection model for curated study of Śrīla Prabhupāda's instructions.
Public pages must only surface records that pass source, rights, and review gates.

## Source-led rules (mandatory)

1. **No fabricated quotes.** Do not invent, paraphrase-as-quote, or AI-generate words attributed to Śrīla Prabhupāda.
2. **No AI impersonation.** Never generate first-person Prabhupāda dialogue, synthetic voice clone of Prabhupāda, or "as if spoken by" content.
3. **No decontextualized quote-card fragments.** Every published excerpt must carry exact source context (date, place/occasion, work or letter locator where applicable) and enough surrounding framing that the teaching is not stripped into a slogan.
4. **Verified locator or it stays private.** Records without an exact edition/locator and rights status remain non-public taxonomy placeholders only.
5. **Correction route required.** Public surfaces must link readers to Knowledge correction / report paths; never present unverified text as settled teaching.

## Taxonomy

- `taxonomy.json` — public shelf categories (Planned until records clear gates) and private editorial facets.
- `records/` — individual record packages. **Empty of real quotations by design for this scaffold.** Only a schema example marked `NOT_FOR_PUBLICATION` may exist.

## Publication boundary

- Taxonomy labels on `/prabhupada-vani` are **Planned** study structure, not published transcripts.
- Moving a record toward public visibility requires the Unified Platform record gates (source dossier, rights, reviews) — never popularity or roadmap pressure.
