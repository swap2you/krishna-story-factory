# Printing and classroom use

Released Krishna Book packages (Stories **001–025**) include a story poster, simple and detailed coloring pages, and an activity PDF. Families and Sunday School teachers can print these for calm, shared practice—without treating Bhāva as an official BBT publication.

## What you can print today

Use the **Printables** hub to browse live assets by story number. For each released story you should find:

- **Story poster** — a single visual for the reading circle or wall.
- **Coloring pages** — simple and detailed line art suitable for younger and older children.
- **Activity sheet (PDF)** — a short worksheet that stays with the story package.

If a puzzle type or extra worksheet is marked **Planned**, it is not ready yet. Do not invent substitute activities that claim to be “from the package.”

## Classroom flow (15–25 minutes)

1. Choose one released story and open its Printables row.
2. Print one poster for the group and one coloring page per child (or share tablets only if your setting already allows screens).
3. Read or play the story narration once, then invite a short coloring quiet time.
4. Close with one sentence of gratitude—no pressure to memorize verses or invent quotes.
5. Send unfinished coloring pages home in a folder; keep posters for the next visit if helpful.

## Permissions and stewardship

- Print for **family, classroom, and Sunday School** use aligned with Bhāva’s Sources and permissions guide.
- Do not scrape, republish, or sell full story text or package archives as your own product.
- Do not present printables as BBT-authorized curriculum.
- Corrections and concerns: use the Knowledge corrections path linked from each record’s trust panel.

## What stays out of scope

Private intake PDFs, scripture golden pages that fail source gates, and any Story **026+** materials are not public. If a resource is missing from Printables, treat it as unavailable—not as a prompt to recreate copyrighted books.
