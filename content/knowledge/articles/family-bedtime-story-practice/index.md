# Family bedtime story practice

Bhāva’s public Library offers child-friendly Krishna Book bedtime stories (Stories **001–025**). This guide is a Bhāva-original practice sheet for parents and caregivers—how to use those stories at home with steadiness and warmth.

It does **not** invent ślokas, purport text, or “quotes from Prabhupāda.” It also does **not** claim official BBT status.

## A simple nightly rhythm (10–20 minutes)

1. **Choose one story** from the Library. Prefer staying with the same story for a few nights if your child asks to hear it again.
2. **Settle the space** — dim lights, put phones away, sit together. Screens are optional; listening to the narration works well when eyes are tired.
3. **Listen or read once** — play the narration or follow along on the story page. Pause if a word is new; keep explanations short and kind.
4. **One soft question** — for example: “What felt peaceful?” or “Who did Krishna help?” Avoid quizzes or pressure to recall every detail.
5. **Close gently** — a moment of quiet, a hug, or a simple “thank you for listening.” Sleep comes more easily without a second media episode.

## Tips that keep practice gentle

- **Sequence matters:** stories are numbered. Younger listeners often do well starting earlier in the series; you do not need to “finish the catalog” quickly.
- **Repeat is devotion, not boredom:** hearing the same story again is normal and welcome.
- **Printables are optional:** a coloring page from the Printables hub can follow on weekends; bedtime itself can stay audio-and-presence only.
- **Boundaries stay honest:** if a story number is not public yet, it will not appear. Do not chase private or incomplete materials.
