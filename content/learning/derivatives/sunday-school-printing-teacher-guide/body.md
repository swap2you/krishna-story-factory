# Sunday School teacher guide: Printing and permissions

> **Status:** Published · Bhāva-original Learning derivative  
> **Primary source:** Knowledge guide `printing-and-classroom-use` (`kl-printables`)  
> **Secondary lineage:** Knowledge guide `source-and-permissions` (`kl-source-permissions`)  
> **Downloads:** none — use this in-page guide; story assets come from the live Printables hub

## Purpose for the teacher

Run one calm Sunday School block that uses **released** Krishna Book printables and states Bhāva’s source boundary honestly: cite carefully, do not republish full books, and do not call Bhāva an official BBT publication.

## Learning objective

Teachers can prepare the session, select real released assets, and explain permissions language without inventing scripture quotes or fake downloads.

## Canonical sources (read before class)

1. `/knowledge/printing-and-classroom-use` — what packages include and how to use Printables.
2. `/knowledge/source-and-permissions` — citation practice and non-republication boundary.
3. `/printables` — live released assets for Stories 001–025 only.
4. Optional planner tools already on site: `/sunday-school`.

## Preparation (10 minutes before class)

- [ ] Pick **one** released story the class already knows or will hear this week.
- [ ] From Printables, open one poster **or** one coloring page for that story (simple or detailed as age-appropriate).
- [ ] Skim Sources and permissions so you can answer “Is this official BBT?” in one honest sentence.
- [ ] Do **not** promise Planned puzzle types, TOP-0147 prayers, or intake PDF bodies — those are blocked or unfinished.

## In-class flow (35–45 minutes)

| Phase | Minutes | What the teacher does | What learners do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 5 | Welcome; light lamp if customary; state today’s story | Settle; greet |
| Story | 10–15 | Read aloud or play package narration | Listen / follow |
| Teach boundary | 5 | From Sources and permissions: Bhāva cites carefully and is not an official BBT publication | Listen; one clarifying question allowed |
| Practice | 10–15 | Hand out / display the chosen printable | Color or discuss one scene calmly |
| Closing | 5 | Recap one kindness theme; send home only real Printables links | Share one sentence they noticed |

## Differentiation

- **Younger / Little Learner:** Shorter listen; simple coloring; one recall question (“Who was protected?”).
- **Older / Teen / Study:** Add the permissions sentence in their own words; optional pair share on why source honesty matters.

## Boundaries

- No invented scripture quotes
- No fake downloads or “pack PDF” claims for this guide (`downloadable: false`)
- Planned festival units and blocked scripture dossiers stay Planned / blocked
- Private Studio and private originals stay private
