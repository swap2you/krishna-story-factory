# Family practice sheet: Printing and classroom use

> **Status:** Published · Bhāva-original Learning derivative  
> **Canonical source:** Knowledge guide `printing-and-classroom-use` (`kl-printables`)  
> **Downloads:** this sheet is not a download pack. Use the live Printables hub for released story assets only.

## Learning objective

Families can find released posters, coloring pages, and activity sheets, pick one calm home activity, and remember that Bhāva materials are not official BBT publications.

## Who this is for

- Parents and guardians practicing with children (ages 5+)
- Sunday School helpers sending home a simple follow-up
- Teachers pointing families to real, already-released assets

## Before you start

1. Open the Knowledge guide: **Printing and classroom use** (`/knowledge/printing-and-classroom-use`).
2. Open the live hub: `/printables`.
3. Choose a **released** Krishna Book story package (Stories 001–025). Do not invent missing puzzle types or claim Planned items are ready.

## Home practice (15–25 minutes)

| Step | What to do | Done? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pick one released story the family already knows or wants to revisit. | ☐ |
| 2 | From Printables, open one poster **or** one coloring page for that story. | ☐ |
| 3 | Listen to the narration once (if available) or read the story page together. | ☐ |
| 4 | Color or talk about one scene — keep the mood calm and affectionate. | ☐ |
| 5 | Ask one gentle question: “What felt kind or protective in this story?” | ☐ |
| 6 | Put materials away respectfully; do not redistribute as if they were official BBT books. | ☐ |

## Family reminder

Released packages may include posters, simple and detailed coloring pages, and activity PDFs. Planned puzzle types stay labeled Planned until reviewed content exists.
