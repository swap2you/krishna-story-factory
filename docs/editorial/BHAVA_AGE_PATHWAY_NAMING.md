# Bhāva Educational Age Pathway Naming

## Purpose

Bhāva uses age-aware educational pathways so families and teachers can find
age-appropriate Krishna Book stories, activities, and source study without
infantilizing older learners.

## Pathway terms

| Educational label | Public title | Intended range | Primary use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaumāra | Little Listeners | through age 5 | Gentle listening, family reading, simple coloring |
| Pāugaṇḍa | Young Explorers | ages 6–10 | Complete stories, activities, guided discussion |
| Kaiśora | Teen Seekers | ages 11–15 | Source reading, reflection, guided philosophy |
| Yauvana | Youth Sevakas | ages 16–20 (site pathway) | Leadership, teaching, research, and service |
| Family Path | Families & Educators | homes & classrooms | Home learning, classrooms, Sunday School support |

## Source rationale

These Sanskrit stage names are inspired by traditional descriptions of
Kṛṣṇa’s age stages found in Vaiṣṇava literature and educational discourse.
They are adopted here only as **pathway labels** for curriculum navigation.

## Non-equivalence disclaimer

These terms are educational pathway names.

They do **not** imply that learners possess Kṛṣṇa’s transcendental qualities,
pastimes, or divine nature. They must never be presented as ontological claims
about children, teens, or adults.

When explaining the terms publicly, prefer wording such as:

> Educational pathways inspired by traditional age-stage language—used only as
> learning labels, not as claims about transcendental qualities.
