## Chapter 7 — The Salvation of Tṛṇāvarta (KB / SB 10.7)

Chronological major and supporting units:

| # | Event unit | Significance | Notes |
|---|------------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | Childhood turning / first birthday (utthāna) ceremony | major (opening) | Band, brāhmaṇas, abhiṣeka; sets cart context |
| 2 | Yaśodā lays hungry Kṛṣṇa beneath the handcart | major | Engaged with guests; misses His cry |
| 3 | Hungry Kṛṣṇa kicks; cart collapses | major | Utensils fall; wheel/axle break |
| 4 | Children report Kṛṣṇa kicked the wheel | supporting→major for fidelity | Adults doubt the children |
| 5 | Adults cannot understand His power | supporting | “Possible and impossible” in His power |
| 6 | Brāhmaṇas’ protective rites; Nanda’s charity | major | Second round of hymns, cows, grain |
| 7 | Kṛṣṇa becomes unbearably heavy in Yaśodā’s lap | major | She places Him on the ground |
| 8 | Tṛṇāvarta arrives as a whirlwind | major | Kaṁsa’s servant |
| 9 | Dust storm; Yaśodā searches, crying | major | Gokula darkened |
| 10 | Kṛṣṇa becomes heavy; catches the demon’s neck | major | Whirlwind stopped in the sky |
| 11 | Tṛṇāvarta falls dead; Kṛṣṇa remains safe | major | Residents recover Him |
| 12 | Residents remember Vasudeva’s warning | supporting | Nanda reflects |
| 13 | While nursing, Kṛṣṇa yawns | major | Distinct from Ch.8 dirt episode |
| 14 | Yaśodā sees the universe in His mouth (first time) | major | First universal-mouth manifestation |

**Chapter 7 must not be reduced to “Tṛṇāvarta only.”** Cart-breaking and the first universal-mouth vision each require full story coverage (or an explicitly reviewed combined mapping — none approved here).

## Provisional Bhāva story bundling (reviewed)

Supporting units may ride inside a dedicated major-story package:

- **010 Cart** — units 1–6 (ceremony through protective rites/charity)
- **011 Tṛṇāvarta** — units 7–12
- **012 First universal mouth** — units 13–14
- **013 Garga / names** — Ch.8 units 1–4
- **014 Crawling** — units 5–7
- **015 Butter complaints** — units 8–9
- **016 Dirt + second universal form** — units 10–15

No combined-story waiver is approved that would merge cart+Tṛṇāvarta, or the two universal mouths, or skip Garga.
