# Future Audio, Podcast, and 3D Roadmap

## Current status

All items in this file are backburner/future phases. They must not add dependencies, tabs, routes, schedulers, or production work to Phase 1.

## Prabhupāda Vāṇī audio/podcast recommendation

NotebookLM can create source-grounded discussions between AI hosts, but those hosts are not Śrīla Prabhupāda. Do **not** create an AI conversation presented as “Prabhupāda and another person,” clone his voice, invent first-person replies, or mix generated dialogue with authentic archival audio without an unmistakable boundary.

Safe format:

- two clearly identified neutral educator hosts discuss an approved lecture/transcript;
- they say “Śrīla Prabhupāda teaches…” and cite the exact source;
- any authentic archival clip is rights-cleared, separately labeled, and not regenerated;
- episode intro/outro discloses AI-generated educational discussion;
- show notes include lecture title/date/place, transcript/source, locators/timecodes, rights, reviewers, and correction route;
- final audio and transcript receive human content, Sanskrit/name, age, devotional, rights, and listening review.

“Latest Prabhupāda lecture” should mean the most recent verified archival recording in the selected collection, not newly generated words.

## Podcast pilot gate

Only after text phases are stable:

1. confirm right to upload each source to a third-party AI service and distribute derivatives;
2. verify transcript and timecodes manually, especially Sanskrit/names/quotations;
3. constrain each notebook to a coherent approved source pack;
4. generate neutral-host audio with an age-specific steering brief;
5. recover/produce a transcript and compare every quote/claim to sources;
6. complete editorial, devotional, age, accessibility, rights, and final listening approval;
7. publish only the immutable approved audio/transcript manifest.

NotebookLM Audio Overviews are AI-hosted summaries and may contain inaccuracies or audio glitches; they are draft artifacts, not evidence.

## Audio phase sequence

- A1: transcript/source/rights model;
- A2: one short neutral-host episode for ages 8–12;
- A3: one teen and one young-adult version from the same canonical dossier;
- A4: transcript equivalence, citations, listening/accessibility QA;
- A5: private owner review;
- A6: scheduler design only after the three-episode pilot passes.

## 3D director/motion lab

Keep separate and offline. Reconsider only when:

- canonical content/page/export standards are locked;
- page production and reviewer capacity are stable;
- character/iconographic/asset rights are governed;
- a reproducible open-source prototype works within hardware/time limits;
- it does not slow the core documentation program.

No temporary mascot, 3D runtime, public navigation item, or cursor-following gimmick.
