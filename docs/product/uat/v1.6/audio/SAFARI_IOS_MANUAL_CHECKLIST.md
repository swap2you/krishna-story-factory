# Safari / iOS manual audio checklist

Use a real Safari (macOS or iOS) against `cursor-v16` URLs:

1. Open `/stories/001` and `/stories/008`
2. Tap Play once; confirm audible playback and Pause appears only after advancement
3. Confirm download link works
4. Background/lock screen: optional Media Session controls
5. Record device, OS, Safari version, pass/fail, timestamp

Do not mark “Safari verified” until this checklist is completed on hardware.
