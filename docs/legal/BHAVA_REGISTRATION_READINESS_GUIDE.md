# Bhāva Registration Readiness Guide

**Operational framework — not legal advice. Do not submit a registration from this guide alone.**

## Registration states

`not_reviewed` · `registration_planned` · `application_prepared` · `submitted` · `registered` · `refused` · `supplementary_registration_needed`

Never claim “registered,” “copyright registered,” or a registration number unless supported by an official record.

## Dossier checklist (manual)

1. Work ID, title, version, SHA-256 inventory  
2. Author / copyright owner public name: Svarna Gauranga Das  
3. Publisher imprint: Dauji Publication  
4. Reviewed first-publication date and country  
5. Deposit copies / exact-eight package archive  
6. AI-assistance and third-party exclusions  
7. Sound-recording claim decision  
8. Pseudonymous / legal-name treatment decision (privacy)  
9. Contact email only — do not expose a home address or phone  

## Manual decisions required before filing

- Confirm first-publication date review  
- Confirm deposit set matches the claimed version  
- Confirm pseudonymous treatment for public records  
- Confirm sound-recording claim (or decline ℗)
