# Bhāva First-Publication Policy

**Operational framework — not legal advice.**

## Track separately

- `creation_date`
- `private_share_date`
- `public_availability_date`
- `first_publication_date`
- `publication_country`
- `registration_status`

## Do not auto-promote

A Git commit, private Drive upload, local portal display, WhatsApp family share, or proof copy must not automatically become the legal first-publication date without review.

## Allowed publication statuses

`draft` · `review` · `privately_shared` · `publicly_available_unreviewed` · `published` · `withdrawn` · `superseded`

A **published** work requires a reviewed `first_publication_date`.

Stories 001–009 copyright retrofit uses status `publicly_available_unreviewed` with `first_publication_date: null` until a steward reviews first publication. Public notices therefore omit a calendar year.

## Year emission rule

No first-publication year may be emitted in consumer notices when `first_publication_date` is unreviewed or absent. Website copyright year for the portal itself is configured separately (`website_copyright_year`).
