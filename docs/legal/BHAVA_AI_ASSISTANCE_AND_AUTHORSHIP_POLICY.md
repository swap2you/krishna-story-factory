# Bhāva AI Assistance and Authorship Policy

**Operational framework — not legal advice.**

## Human authorship

Manifests must identify the human-authored contribution. Prompting alone is not authorship.

## Disclosure

Record provider, model, voice, image model, human editing/production, and claim limitations in the package `rights` / `ai_assistance` block.

## Images

When imagery is AI-assisted, prefer a limited design/publication credit rather than a full-image copyright overclaim, and record the limitation in the manifest.

## Audio / sound recording

For AI/TTS narration, record text authorship, human editing and production, voice/TTS provider, provider terms snapshot/date when available, AI assistance, and `sound_recording_claim_status`.

Do not publish a ℗ line unless status is `approved` after manual rights review. Default for current Stories 001–009 TTS packages: `needs_manual_review`.
