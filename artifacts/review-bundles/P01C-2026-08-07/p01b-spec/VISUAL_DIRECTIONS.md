# Visual Directions — Three Boards (placeholders only)

**D07:** Boards produced for owner selection. **OD-01:** choose one before asset work.  
**Shared brand:** deep navy `#061628`, muted gold `#d4a84b`, ivory/cream `#f7f0e4` / `#fffaf2`; optional restrained saffron/lotus/tulasi.
